2. Overview

This site processes personal data only where technically necessary or when you contact us. The Human Design app stores profile data locally in your browser (localStorage) — no server-side sync.

3. Hosting

Static pages are served via GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). Connection logs (IP, timestamp, user agent) may be processed. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure operation). Data may be transferred to the US under standard contractual clauses.

4. Local storage in /app/

The app stores the following only in your browser (localStorage, key e.g. hd-profile-db-v2):

  • Birth data and calculated chart data
  • Profile names and team selection (Penta)
  • Optional OEJTS/Jung test answers
  • Language preference and transit cache

You can delete this via browser settings. Nothing is sent to us unless you contact us separately.

5. Google Fonts

Fonts are loaded from Google; your IP may be transmitted to Google Ireland / Google LLC (US). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. You may block Google Fonts via browser extensions.

6. Swiss Ephemeris (in-browser WASM)

Chart calculations run entirely client-side. Birth input is not sent to our servers for calculation.

7. Contact via email or WhatsApp

We process contact data to handle your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). WhatsApp is operated by Meta; data may be transferred internationally. Use WhatsApp only if you accept this.

8. Coaching and payment

For booked readings we process additional data (name, payment reference) only as needed for contract performance.

9. Retention

Contact messages are deleted when no longer needed, at latest after 3 years unless legal retention applies.
