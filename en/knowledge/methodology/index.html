Methodology — ephemeris to bodygraph

Clear separation: astronomical calculation, HD model, interpretation. Daniel Klotz.

1. Input

Birth date, time (with timezone), location (lat/lon).

2. Swiss Ephemeris (client-side)

Planetary positions computed in-browser via WASM — no birth data sent to our servers.

3. Gate & line mapping

Ecliptic longitude → 64-gate wheel, 6 lines per gate. Personality and Design (~88° solar arc before birth) computed separately.

4. Centers, channels, type

Defined centers from active channels. Type from sacral/throat configuration. Authority from center hierarchy.

5. Verification

Repository includes verify-hd-engine.py and reference charts.

What we do not claim

  • Gate texts are clinically proven personality traits
  • Transits causally trigger events
  • The app provides medical or psychological diagnosis
