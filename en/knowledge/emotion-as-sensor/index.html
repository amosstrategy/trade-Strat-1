Emotion as sensor

With a defined Solar Plexus, the emotional wave may act as a timing sensor — wait until clarity emerges through the wave.

The hypothesis

  1. Defined Solar Plexus produces a recurring emotional wave.
  2. Phases include enthusiasm, doubt, clarity, fatigue — observable, not perfectly predictable.
  3. Major decisions should feel "yes" at multiple points on the wave, not only at peaks.
  4. Open Solar Plexus: amplify others' emotions — calibrate with "Is this mine?"

Evidence level

Strong in HD practice literature; not established clinical evidence. Proband observation planned without healing claims.
