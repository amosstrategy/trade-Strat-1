Big Five — IPIP-NEO-120 in the app

Paradise Ventures uses the Five-Factor Model (OCEAN) exclusively via the full IPIP-NEO-120 by John Johnson (2014) — Public Domain, no NEO-PI-R license, no short form (no IPIP-50).

What's integrated

  • 120 items — full test only — 5 domains, 30 facets; scoring and export require 120/120
  • Big Five tab in the Daily Energy Map — per profile, local storage
  • Percentiles — approximated from Johnson internet-sample norms
  • HD feature vector — type, authority, centers, channels (exploratory)
  • Admin export — ?admin=1 → JSON for proband phase

Why IPIP not NEO-PI-R?

NEO-PI-R is commercial. IPIP items are Public Domain. Our planned study is regression-based: HD features → IPIP facets — not categorical type matching.
