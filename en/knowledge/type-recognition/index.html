Type recognition — mechanical, not mystical

HD type follows defined centers and sacral–throat wiring. It describes energy economics, not character.

Five types

Generator — defined sacral; strategy: respond.

Manifesting Generator — sacral + motor to throat; still reactive at core.

Projector — no sacral; strategy: wait for invitation.

Manifestor — motor to throat, no sacral; strategy: inform before initiating.

Reflector — no defined centers; strategy: lunar cycle (~28 days) for big decisions.
