# Diving, spearfishing and underwater traps — private use rules  
**Thailand · Vietnam · Indonesia · Australia · Philippines** (+ Denmark note for the “random beach” idea)

**Stand:** July 2026  
**Purpose:** Can you buy gear and go alone? Spear? Trap? Scuba or freedive? What blocks becoming an instructor if you only free-dive today?  
**Disclaimer:** Not legal advice. Fisheries and marine-park rules change by province/state and year — confirm with local fisheries office / dive shop / park authority before entering the water with a spear or trap.

---

## 1. Short answers to your questions

| Question | Short answer |
|----------|--------------|
| Can I just buy diving gear and go? | **Scuba for sightseeing:** usually yes with proper training/certification and local site rules. **Scuba + spear:** almost everywhere in your list **no**. |
| Spear on a normal non-reserve beach? | **Sometimes**, if: salt/tidal waters open, **breath-hold only**, license/fee where required, species/size limits, and **not** a swimming/exclusion zone. “Normal beach” is often still closed or restricted (e.g. NSW ocean beaches almost fully closed to spear). |
| Can I fish with a spear? | Treat as **fishing**, not as diving sport. Rules of fisheries law apply. |
| Underwater trap while diving? | Separate gear rules. Usually **tagged pots only**, species-specific licences (esp. lobster), often **illegal general fish traps**. Scuba + “drop any trap I like” = commonly illegal. |
| Same rules with “complete diving gear”? | **Breathing gas changes everything.** Snorkel OK for spear almost everywhere legal. **Scuba/hookah + taking fish** is banned in Thailand practice, Indonesia practice, Australia states checked, Philippines (DOT sport-diving rules), Denmark/EU, and often treated as unfair / illegal elsewhere. |
| Copenhagen-style casual beach? | Denmark: **saltwater freedive spear OK** with **fisketegn**, **no scuba**, daylight only, size/season/protection belts. Not “anything goes.” |

**Core pattern across all five target countries:**  
**Private scuba diving ≠ private spearfishing.** And **tourism MPAs ≠ open coast.**

## 2. What you need privately (minimum kit vs legal kit)

### A) Scuba sightseeing only (no spear, no take)

Typical private path:

1. Medical fitness (questionnaire / doctor if needed).  
2. Certification (Open Water or equivalent) — shops and many boats will not fill tanks or take you without a card.  
3. Local dive briefing / boat waiver; marine-park tickets where required.  
4. Gear: BCD, regulator, computer, weights, suit — own or rent.  
5. Buddy practice and gas planning (standards of your training agency).

You can own the equipment. You still need the **skill card** for safe and practical access.

### B) Spearfishing (private)

Usually:

1. **Freedive / apnea skills** (often more important than scuba).  
2. Speargun or polespear (muscle-powered; gas “powerheads” often banned).  
3. Float / floatline / dive flag where required or recommended.  
4. Fishing licence / recreational fee where the country or state requires it (**Australia NSW**, **Denmark** — clear; Thailand recreational often none nationally).  
5. Knowledge of: bag/size limits, protected species, closed zones, night rules.

### C) Traps / pots

Usually **not** covered by a dive certificate. Separate **fisheries** licences, number limits, float marking, soak times. Scuba may be allowed to check pots or hand-take lobster **only** where that state’s dive-lobster rules allow — never as a free-for-all.

## 3. Country comparison

Legend:  
**🟢** Allowed under clear rules · **🟡** Grey / local / licence · **🔴** Banned or effectively banned for visitors

### Thailand

| Activity | Status | Notes |
|----------|--------|--------|
| Own scuba, recreational dive | 🟢 | Need training; parks (Similan, Phi Phi, etc.) have fees and strict behaviour rules (no coral contact etc.). |
| Spearfishing breath-hold, outside parks | 🟡/🟢 | Widely described as allowed for personal use; enforcement uneven; avoid busy snorkel sites. |
| Spearfishing + **scuba** | 🔴 | Consistently treated as prohibited (fair-chase / fisheries practice). |
| Spear inside marine national parks | 🔴 | Fishing banned; assembled gun in park often treated as serious. |
| Protected species (e.g. parrotfish in Phuket rules, sharks, turtles, clams) | 🔴 | Heavy fines / jail risk. |
| Small traditional traps (personal, outside parks) | 🟡 | Thai fisheries texts list some traditional traps for small-scale use — **tourist “scuba + random trap” is not the same**; parks and “efficient” gear bans still apply. Confirm locally. |
| National recreational spear licence | 🟢 often none | Still subject to provincial and park law. |

**Private takeaway:** Gear shopping alone does **not** unlock scuba-hunting. Freedive spear outside parks may be possible; treat every tourist reef as **probably off-limits** until a local operator maps it.

### Vietnam

| Activity | Status | Notes |
|----------|--------|--------|
| Recreational scuba | 🟢/🟡 | Certification + local operators; some sites restricted. |
| Spearfishing | 🟡 | Generally said to be legal recreationally; **strongly local**. |
| MPAs / parks (e.g. parts of Phu Quoc, Ha Long-type protections) | 🔴 | Spearfishing prohibited. |
| Fisheries Law 2017 | 🟡 | Commercial vessels (≥6 m etc.) need fishing licences; banned methods/species lists apply; recreational spear sits in a **grey interpretive** zone — check provincial fisheries. |
| Scuba + spear | 🟡→🔴 | Not as cleanly published as Australia/Thailand bans in English; **do not assume legal**. Local bans and banned-gear lists can cover it. |
| Import speargun | 🟡 | May be treated like a weapon at customs — declare. |
| Underwater traps | 🟡/🔴 | Passive harvesting is regulated; random recreational trap sets are high-risk without a licence. |

**Private takeaway:** Vietnam is the **least clear** of the four for DIY spear. Do not improvise from a beach near a famous snorkeling area.

### Indonesia

| Activity | Status | Notes |
|----------|--------|--------|
| Recreational scuba | 🟢 | Huge training industry (Bali, Komodo corridors, etc.). |
| Spearfishing freedive (e.g. Bali open areas) | 🟡/🟢 | Often described as legal for visitors outside protected zones. |
| Spearfishing as tourist in **Raja Ampat MPA** | 🔴 | Explicit: tourists/visitors may **not** spearfish; locals may use traditional *jubi* / *senapan molo* only in allowed fishery zones. |
| Scuba + spear / take | 🔴 | Explicitly prohibited in Raja Ampat regs; widely banned in practice elsewhere. |
| Speargun vs traditional gear | 🟡 | Zoning documents treat “speargun” and traditional weapons differently; tourists do not get local customary rights. |
| Traps | 🟡/🔴 | Recreational general traps often restricted; MPAs stricter. |

**Private takeaway:** Indonesia is **map-dependent**. Bali open coast ≠ Raja Ampat. Buying a gun in a shop does not grant MPA rights.

### Australia (state-based — check the state you land in)

| Activity | NSW (example) | Queensland (example) | Pattern |
|----------|---------------|----------------------|---------|
| Recreational fishing fee / licence | 🟢 Required for spear | Rec rules apply | Almost always a **fee/licence** layer |
| Spearfishing | 🟢 tidal saltwater where not closed | 🟢 selected tidal waters | **Inland/fresh often banned** |
| Scuba / hookah while taking fish | 🔴 (scuba only for scallops/urchins) | 🔴 UBA other than snorkel banned for taking fish | National pattern: **no scuba hunting** |
| Ocean beaches | Mostly 🔴 (except last 20 m each end in NSW) | Closed waters lists | “Normal beach” often illegal for spear |
| Lobster / dive take | Hand only; separate rules | Hand / limited gear | Spears for lobster usually illegal |
| Recreational traps | Species-specific | Up to 4 permitted trap types, **tagged**, float rules | Random unlabelled fish cage = illegal |
| Selling catch | 🔴 recreational | 🔴 | Same |

**Private takeaway:** Australia is the **most explicit**. DIY beach-spear with scuba is a short path to a fine. Always use the state DPI/DAF page + marine park maps.

### Denmark note (your “Copenhagen beach” example)

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Licence | **Fisketegn** (angling licence) for spearing as sport fishing |
| Water | **Saltwater only** — freshwater spear illegal |
| Breathing | **No scuba** while spearing (EU tech measures + national practice since 2019) |
| When | **Daylight only** (dawn–dusk) for spear/projectile hunting |
| Gun type | Muscle-powered / hand-loaded; externally gas-pressurised guns not allowed |
| Zones | 500 m river-mouth protection belts, harbours, species/size/season |

So even at a “normal” Danish beach: licence + freedive + rules — not gear-from-shop, go.

### Philippines

| Activity | Status | Notes |
|----------|--------|--------|
| Own scuba, recreational dive | 🟢 | Major training hubs (Anilao, Puerto Galera, Moalboal, Malapascua, Coron, etc.). DOT licence rules for dive *businesses*; private cert + local shop/boat is the normal path. |
| Spearfishing breath-hold | 🟡 | Practised culturally, but **municipal waters are LGU-controlled**. Conservation bodies cite RA 10654 §95 (“active gear” in municipal waters) as covering spear/speargun unless the LGU designates spearfishing zones. Do **not** assume open coast = legal. |
| Spearfishing + **scuba** / compressor | 🔴 | DOT *Rules and Regulations Governing the Business of Sport Scuba Diving* (2009): spearfishing using scuba is a **prohibited activity** for dive establishments/professionals. Compressor/scuba fishing also widely banned under Fisheries Code practice and local ordinances. 2025 CMAS case: divemaster revoked after scuba spear in a declared marine sanctuary (Cathedral Rocks / Anilao). |
| Marine sanctuaries / MPAs / no-take | 🔴 | Fishing of any kind banned (e.g. Tubbataha: no fishing; gear must be stowed on entry). Thousands of local fish sanctuaries — maps differ by barangay/municipality. |
| Speargun vs traditional spear | 🟡 | Some LGUs ban spearguns while allowing traditional handheld spears for residents; tourists rarely get customary rights. |
| Fish traps / pens / cages (*bubo*, etc.) | 🔴 for DIY tourist | RA 8550 §51: traps/pens/cages only in **LGU-designated zones** with **licence**. Random underwater trap while diving = illegal. |
| National recreational spear licence | 🟡 often none nationally | **Municipal fishing permit** / LGU ordinance still bite; “no national card” ≠ free rein. |
| Selling catch as visitor | 🔴 | Commercial/municipal fishing needs permits; recreational tourist sale is not the model. |

**Private takeaway:** The Philippines is **easy for scuba sightseeing**, hard for “buy a gun and spear the tourist reef.” Freedive spear may exist where an LGU allows it — ask the **municipal agriculture / fisheries office**, not only a hostel. Scuba + spear is a career-ender for dive pros and a clear ban under DOT sport-diving rules.

## 5. From no-gear free-diver → diving instructor

Spearfishing law and instructor career are **separate tracks**.

### Scuba instructor path (typical PADI; SSI/RAID similar idea)

1. **Open Water** → **Advanced** → **Rescue**  
2. **Divemaster** (≥40–60 logged dives during path; 60+ common before DM certify)  
3. **IDC** (Assistant Instructor + OWSI) → **Instructor Exam (IE)**  
4. Need ~**100 logged dives** for IE, medical, first aid/CPR, EFR Instructor often during IDC  
5. Cost order of magnitude: rough **USD 3,000–7,000+** all-in depending on location (Thailand IDCs often cheaper packages)

Your current **no-equipment breath diving** helps **water comfort and apnea** (huge for spearfishing and for working as a free-diving instructor). It does **not** skip scuba coursework: buoyancy with tanks, gas management, and teaching standards are a new skill set.

### Parallel: free-diving instructor

If you love apnea more than tanks: **PADI Freediver / SSI / AIDA** instructor path. Often a better fit for spearfishing communities than scuba OWSI — and still requires formal courses, not “I free-dive alone therefore I teach.”

### Parallel career: industrial / commercial diver

If the goal is **Industrie-Taucher** (not sport instructor): PADI/SSI tickets do **not** qualify. See separate note: `reports/industrie-taucher-jobs-lizenzen-2026.md` (Germany IHK path, IMCA/ADAS/HSE/ADCI, where demand is hottest 2025–26).

### Can you “adapt”?

Yes. Many instructors started as strong swimmers/free divers. Plan:

1. Get Open Water + build **logged** dives (quality, not only depth bravado).  
2. Keep spearfishing on **freedive** days only — never mix tanks + gun where banned.  
3. Build local legal knowledge as a professional duty (guests will ask to spear on scuba — your job is to refuse when illegal).  
4. Pick a base country whose employment visa + agency recognition you can live with (Thailand/Philippines training hubs vs Australia work rights are very different). The Philippines is one of the densest instructor-training markets in Asia — good for logged dives and cheap package IDCs, but work as a foreign instructor still needs immigration compliance.

## 6. Practical “don’t get fined” checklist

Before any private spear or trap session:

1. Is this a **national park / MPA / no-take** zone? If unsure → treat as closed.  
2. Am I on **breath-hold only**? If wearing a tank → **no take**.  
3. Do I need a **fishing fee/licence** today?  
4. Species: protected / minimum size / bag limit?  
5. Beach/swimmer buffer / harbour rules?  
6. Trap: legal type, **name tag**, float, licence number?  
7. Customs: declaring a speargun on entry?  
8. Can I sell? Almost always **no** as a recreational visitor.

---

## 7. Bottom line

- **Private scuba sightseeing:** doable with training; buy gear if you want.  
- **Private spearfishing:** usually **freedive-only**, zone-limited, sometimes licensed.  
- **Scuba + spear on a random beach:** answer for your five countries is effectively **no**.  
- **Philippines extra twist:** even freedive spear can be illegal in municipal waters unless the local government authorised zones — LGU ordinances beat “it looks like a normal beach.”  
- **Underwater traps:** not a free add-on to dive gear — **fisheries licences and gear standards**.  
- **Instructor dream:** start the **certified scuba ladder** (or free-dive instructor ladder) soon; your apnea background is an advantage, not a shortcut past standards.
