Circular economy · Kreislauf statt Pendel

Natural systems circulate. Sick systems swing.

This lens runs through my work — markets, medicine, relationships, Human Design. I help you spot when life is in flow versus when you're stuck between two poles pretending one must win.

The cycle

Phases feed the next. Rest recharges work. Symbiosis beats parasitism. Abundance moves — it doesn't hoard. This is what "thriving together" actually looks like in a body, a team, a day.

The pendulum

Two poles fighting. A wins, then B. Extraction, burnout, false either/or. In HD: living someone else's definition, or deciding from the field instead of your authority.

The move is not to pick a side harder — it's to dissolve the swing into a circle. That's what a reading is for: seeing your mechanics so you stop penduluming against your own design.
