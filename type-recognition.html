Type Recognition Through Emotion

Reference guide · Personality (inner view) × Design (outer view) · Early-warning stages · Community diagnosis
Emotion is a sensor, not an identity. It produces a hypothesis about type, not a diagnosis — the chart comes from birth data. This tool reads from two sides: how it feels from the inside (Personality — what you sense yourself) and how it looks from the outside (Design — what others observe). Together they form the full picture, because people often see themselves differently from how they come across.
■ Inside = what the person feels and asks themselves (Personality side).   ■ Outside = what you can observe about them in the room (Design side).   Each card has two progressions: the green blooming stages (how the right thing grows, from first sign to full power) and the early-warning stages (how it tips, from first sign to too late). Goal: recognize and nourish green growth early; catch the tipping early, on the left.

The five types

GeneratorAura: open & enveloping~37%

Recognition · Language & questions

Outside: responds to offers instead of starting on their own. Answers with sounds (mm-hmm / nah) before words come. Asks “does this light me up?”, “do I want in?”. Steady energy when they love what they do.

Drive

Inside: needs a counterpart, a question, something to respond to. Starting from nothing feels wrong. The gut knows yes/no immediately.

In power · Blooming stages (Signature: Satisfaction)

1 SignCalm returns. Responds again instead of forcing. First curiosity.
2 FlowSteady energy on one thing. Loses track of time while doing.
3 MasteryGets genuinely good. Others come with offers because they radiate.
4 Full powerTired-satisfied in the evening. A full “that was it.” Draws life in.

Visible in the environment: the steady hum, no rush, no forcing. Amplify: give them real options their gut can answer to.

Out of power · Early-warning stages (Not-Self: Frustration)

1 NoiseRestlessness, impatience. “Something’s off.” No name for it yet.
2 SignalActive frustration. Pushes harder at the wrong thing.
3 AlarmResignation. “Just do what you’re told.” Gut goes quiet.
4 Too lateBurnout, flatness, depression-like.

Visible in the environment from stage 1: the steadily energized one becomes restless-irritable. Intervene: “What did you actually say yes to here — and did you want that?”

Manifesting GeneratorAura: open & enveloping~33%

Recognition · Language & questions

Outside: does several things at once, skips steps, drops what feels wrong. Fast, impatient with slowness. Asks “can’t this go faster?”

Drive

Inside: gut-yes plus urge to act immediately. Quitting isn’t failure — it’s sorting out the wrong path. Must briefly inform before charging ahead.

In power · Blooming stages (Signature: Satisfaction + Peace)

1 SignGut-yes plus a brief inform. No more charging off.
2 FlowMulti-track, but directed. Skips steps others need.
3 MasteryFinds the fastest path for everyone. Becomes the group’s accelerator.
4 Full powerSatisfaction + clear runway. Does in hours what others take days for.

Visible: fast, but no longer frantic — the pace now serves. Amplify: let them take shortcuts without insisting on the “right order.”

Out of power · Early-warning stages (Not-Self: Frustration + Anger)

1 NoiseRestlessness + irritability. Starts things, doesn’t finish.
2 SignalFrustration (wrong thing) tips into anger (blocked, not informed).
3 AlarmDrops everything, even the right thing. Scorched earth.
4 Too lateBurnout + resentment at once. Torn apart.

Double signal — that’s why it’s intense. In the environment: becomes frantic AND snippy at the same time. Intervene: “Which of these do you really want — and who do you need to inform?”

ProjectorAura: focused & penetrating~20%

Recognition · Language & questions

Outside: sees through others, offers insights, asks deep questions about you. In groups, sits slightly at the edge until invited. Asks “may I add something?”, “do you see that too?”

Drive

Inside: waits for invitation/recognition, quickly feels exhaustion in groups (absorbs others’ energy). Longs to be seen for who they really are.

In power · Blooming stages (Signature: Success)

1 SignStops pushing in. Waits, rests in themselves. No sarcasm.
2 InvitationGets asked. Their insight lands because it was requested.
3 ImpactGuides without pushing. People seek their view of them.
4 SuccessRecognized for who they really are. Seen, not spent.

Visible: relaxed at the edge, present rather than pushing, sharp gaze without bitterness. Amplify: invite them explicitly and ask for their perspective.

Out of power · Early-warning stages (Not-Self: Bitterness)

1 NoiseSarcasm. Pointed comments, unsolicited “tips,” still charming.
2 SignalBitterness. “Nobody sees me. I give and give.” Personal.
3 AlarmWithdrawal + superiority. “I’m above all this.”
4 Too lateHealth collapse (worked like a Generator).

With bitterness, it’s already too late. The first tangible signal is sarcasm — intervene here: “Where are you giving right now without anyone asking you?”

ManifestorAura: closed & repelling~9%

Recognition · Language & questions

Outside: starts things from nothing, comes across as independent, hard to read. Others sometimes feel unconsciously pushed away. Doesn’t ask much — announces. “I’m doing this now.”

Drive

Inside: inner impulse to initiate, wants a clear runway, hates control. Must inform (not ask), or resistance shows up.

In power · Blooming stages (Signature: Peace)

1 SignInforms on their own before starting. The fuse gets longer.
2 Clear runwayAnnounces — no resistance comes. Moves unhindered.
3 ImpulseSets in motion what no one else starts. Others follow the impulse.
4 PeaceCalm, independent, undisturbed. Acts without having to fight.

Visible: initiatory, but no longer abrupt — shares instead of ambushing. Amplify: don’t control; being informed is enough, then give space.

Out of power · Early-warning stages (Not-Self: Anger)

1 NoiseIrritability, short fuse, impatience with “brakes.”
2 SignalExplosive anger. “Just let me DO it.” (not informed → resistance)
3 AlarmCold withdrawal. Shuts everyone out. “I’ll do it alone.”
4 Too lateParanoia pattern. Sees resistance everywhere.

In the environment: becomes abrupt and shuts down. Intervene: “Who didn’t you inform before you went ahead?”

ReflectorAura: permeable & sampling~1%

Recognition · Language & questions

Outside: mirrors the group, seems different every day, remarkably wise about the community’s climate. Asks “how are we all doing?”, speaks about the group as a whole.

Drive

Inside: takes everything in, needs ~28 days (lunar cycle) for big decisions. Feels most at ease in the right place with the right people.

In power · Blooming stages (Signature: Surprise)

1 SignTakes time, no longer jumps. Lets the lunar cycle run.
2 Right placeFinds the right people, the right environment. Feels held.
3 MirrorClearly mirrors the group’s climate back. Becomes the wise voice of the whole.
4 SurpriseMarvels at the unexpected. Their well-being = the group is healthy.

Visible: lights up, speaks lovingly about the community. Their blooming is the fever thermometer — if they’re well, everyone is well. Amplify: give them time and keep the environment healthy.

Out of power · Early-warning stages (Not-Self: Disappointment)

1 NoiseQuiet dissatisfaction. “Is this really all there is?”
2 SignalJumps. Switches group, place, identity — searching for an image that holds.
3 AlarmCynicism. “Nothing is real; everyone’s playing roles.”
4 Too lateIdentity crisis. Confuses themselves with what was mirrored.

The Reflector is the early-warning sensor for the whole community. Their disappointment often measures the group, not them. Intervene: “What is our community missing right now?”

The second axis · Source or receiver

Beyond type: does the emotion run from the inside or from the outside? Defined Solar Plexus (~50%) = own wave, high↔low in their own rhythm, independent of the room — sends. Open Solar Plexus (~50%, all Reflectors + most Projectors) = absorbs and amplifies others’ emotion — receives, goes quiet alone. Test in the environment: Does the person’s mood tip with the room and settle when alone? → open. Do they run their own up-and-down no matter who’s there? → defined. This crosses with type (a Generator can be emotional or not; a Projector too) — only Reflectors are always open.
Important for recognizing others: An open Projector or Reflector can show all four Not-Self emotions in turn, because they mirror the field. The switching itself is the clue to “open.” Only Generator/MG (frustration) and pure Manifestor (anger) show stable, own signals. With the others, separate mirror from self first: What remains when the person is alone?

Community diagnosis · Reading the emotional climate

◇ From individual types to the group picture

A community is healthy when each type lives their Signature, not their Not-Self. You don’t need to know charts to read the climate — the emotions in the room reveal it. Scan the group for these patterns:

When you see in the room……the community is
Satisfied makers, heard advisors, calm starters, marveling mirrorsHealthy. Everyone on their path. Circulation — energy flows, no one against anyone.
Many frustrated GeneratorsThe group forces people into wrong work / yes without gut. Structure problem: wrong roles.
Sarcasm & bitterness (Projectors)The guides aren’t invited/heard. Knowledge dissipates. Early signal = sarcasm — there’s still time.
Bottled anger & cold withdrawals (Manifestors)Too much control, too little information. Initiators get blocked instead of informed.
Jumping, cynical ReflectorsAlarm level for the WHOLE group. The Reflector measures the collective — their disappointment is your fever thermometer.

The adaptive rule: Always intervene at the earliest stage you see — not when it blows up. Sarcasm before bitterness. Irritability before explosion. Restlessness before burnout. “Is this all?” before identity crisis. And watch the Reflector first — they show the climate before the others can name it.

The circulation lever: A tipping community oscillates (A against B, camp against camp, extractive). A healthy one circulates (each type nourishes the next: Manifestor starts → Generators build → Projectors guide → Reflector mirrors back). The task isn’t to suppress emotions, but to lead each person back to their Signature. Then abundance flows for everyone, instead of oscillating between winners and losers.
