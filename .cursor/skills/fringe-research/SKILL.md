# Fringe Research — Buried Evidence Finder

Method for surfacing real but hard-to-find research on anomalous/fringe topics, across five
languages, with strict evidence separation. The goal is the FULL picture (proven, contested,
debunked, speculative) cleanly labeled, never blurred together. Output language: German.
Search languages: English, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian.

## Prime directives (never skip)

1. **The whole picture, cleanly separated.** Show proven, contested, debunked and speculative
   side by side, but never let them blur. Every claim gets a tier label (see Evidence Ampel).
2. **Abstract ≠ study.** Abstracts routinely oversell. Whenever possible, check the actual
   results/figures against the abstract's claims. Flag mismatches explicitly, they are common
   and important. If only the abstract is accessible, say so and mark the finding as unverified.
3. **No absence of criticism without suspicion.** If a claim has NO counterarguments, critics,
   or failed replications anywhere, treat that as a red flag (echo chamber, no serious scrutiny),
   not as strength. Say so.
4. **Reputation is data.** Name who did the work and their standing: credentialed insiders,
   career fringe researchers, or qualified cross-field entrants (someone with a real hard-science
   background who moved into the topic, often the most interesting). Note funding and affiliation.
5. **Skip the generic.** No Wikipedia summaries, no listicles, no SEO wellness blogs as sources.
   Go to primary literature, preprints, theses, conference proceedings, declassified archives,
   and regional-language repositories that English Google buries.

## The Evidence Ampel (always apply)

Label every finding with one tier. Use these exact German labels in output:

- **🟢 BELEGT** — replicated in peer-reviewed work, or meta-analysis with defensible methods.
  Rare in this field. Reserve it.
- **🟡 UMSTRITTEN** — real published data, but small samples, replication problems, or active
  scientific dispute. Most serious fringe research lives here.
- **🟠 GRAU** — grey literature: conference abstracts, dissertations, declassified government
  research, Soviet/Russian institute reports, preprints without review. Real documents, unvetted.
- **🔴 WIDERLEGT** — failed blinded replication or exposed methodological/fraud problems.
  Still include it (debunked cases matter), but label clearly and explain WHY it failed.
- **⚪ SPEKULATION** — interesting hypothesis, no solid data yet. Mark as such, never smuggle in.

## Where to look (not Google's first page)

**Academic / preprint:**
PubMed, Europe PMC, Semantic Scholar, CORE.ac.uk, BASE (base-search.net), OpenAlex, bioRxiv,
PsyArXiv, ResearchGate, Academia.edu, Google Scholar (use, but dig past page 1),
Journal of Scientific Exploration (JSE), Explore (NY), Frontiers, PLOS.

**Fringe-specific but methodical:**
Society for Psychical Research (SPR) archives, Parapsychological Association proceedings,
Rhine Research Center, IONS (Institute of Noetic Sciences) publications, Global Consciousness
Project data, Division of Perceptual Studies (UVA, DOPS).

**Declassified / government:**
CIA FOIA Reading Room (Stargate / remote viewing — thousands of real declassified docs),
DIA, DTIC, national archives. Soviet-era: search the institute names directly.

**Regional-language repositories (this is where the buried gold is):**
- RU: CyberLeninka (cyberleninka.ru), eLibrary.ru, dissercat.com, Soviet institute reports
- ES: SciELO, Dialnet, Redalyc
- PT: SciELO Brasil, Biblioteca Digital de Teses e Dissertações (BDTD)
- DE: DNB, base-search.net, German university repositories (OPUS), MPG.PuRe

## Five-language search protocol

For each topic, run the query in all five languages, because each research culture published
different things that don't cross-index. Translate the concept, don't just transliterate.

Example — "twin physiological connectedness":
- EN: "twin telepathy" EDA, "physiological connectedness" twins, "remote staring" autonomic
- DE: Zwillinge Telepathie Hautleitwert, "physiologische Verbundenheit" Zwillinge
- ES: gemelos telepatía conductancia, conexión fisiológica gemelos
- PT: gêmeos telepatia condutância, conexão fisiológica gêmeos
- RU: близнецы телепатия кожно-гальваническая, дистанционное взаимодействие близнецы

Use each language's own terminology for the field. RU parapsychology terms differ sharply from
EN (e.g. "дистанционное взаимодействие", "биоэнергоинформатика"). ES/PT often use
"parapsicología/parapsicologia", "percepción extrasensorial".

## Search operators that beat the generic

- `filetype:pdf` + topic + author → skips blogs, lands on papers/theses
- `site:cia.gov/readingroom` + topic → declassified
- `site:cyberleninka.ru` or `site:scielo.br` + topic in that language
- exact-phrase quotes for the specific effect name, not the popular name
- author-name chaining: find one real researcher, then search their name + "et al" for the
  whole citation network around them
- year ranges to find the ORIGINAL study, not the 400 blogs citing it secondhand

## Verification workflow (per finding)

1. Find the primary source (not a news article about it).
2. Read beyond the abstract. Pull the actual N, effect size, p-value, whether blinded, whether
   pre-registered. Compare to what the abstract/headline claims. Note any gap.
3. Search for replication attempts and for named critics. Search the critic's name too.
4. Identify the researcher: credentials, field, cross-over background, institution, funding.
   A hard-science PhD who crossed into the topic is worth flagging as higher-signal than a
   career believer or a pure debunker.
5. Assign the Ampel tier. If no criticism exists anywhere → flag the suspicious silence.

## Output format (German)

For each topic deliver:

1. **Kurzantwort** — 2–3 sentences: what's actually established vs. hyped.
2. **Nach Evidenz-Stufe getrennt** — findings grouped under 🟢🟡🟠🔴⚪, each with: the specific
   study/document, N and method in one line, who did it + reputation, and the abstract-vs-result
   check if done.
3. **Die vergrabene Perle** — the one hard-to-find source the person couldn't have googled.
4. **Wo du mit VPN selbst nachschauen kannst** — regional links worth checking from-country.
5. **Primärquellen** — full citations.

## Known high-value trailheads (starting points, not conclusions)

- Twin/physiological connectedness: Jensen & Parker (Explore 2012/2013), Brusewitz & Parker
  (2024), Ramakers/Stevens/Morris (2005). 🟡/🟠
- Remote viewing: CIA Stargate declassified corpus; SRI (Puthoff, Targ). 🟠, mixed.
- Presentiment: Bem (2011, controversial), Mossbridge meta-analysis (2012). 🟡/🔴 split.
- Global consciousness / RNG: Nelson, GCP / PEAR lab (Jahn, Princeton). 🟡/🔴 disputed.
- Water: Emoto (🔴 debunked, no blinded replication — keep for debunking); Montagnier water
  "memory" (🔴/🟠, disputed); structured-water claims (⚪/🔴).
- Energy healing: Bengston (mice, intriguing 🟡); Reiki RCTs (mostly 🔴/placebo).
- NDE / consciousness: DOPS (UVA), Parnia AWARE study (🟡).

Always re-verify — these are trailheads, current status may have shifted.
